Agri Ledger

Smart Agriculture Record & Farm Management System

Agri Ledger is a digital platform designed to help farmers and landowners manage agricultural records and financial activities easily.

Key Features

- Farmer Records Management
- Land and Acreage Records
- Crop and Harvest Records
- Expense and Sales Management
- Inventory Management
- Income and Profit/Loss Calculation
- Farmer and Landowner Accounting
- Reports and Bill Management
- English, Urdu, and Sindhi Language Support

Technologies Used

- React
- TypeScript
- Vite
- Tailwind CSS
- Supabase

Project Purpose

The goal of Agri Ledger is to simplify agricultural record-keeping and financial management through a digital solution.
